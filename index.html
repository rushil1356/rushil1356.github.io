ABOUT

I care about the layer where engineering decisions become user experience. I like taking a real person's problem and shipping something concrete enough to test.

I'm a Computer Science junior at Rutgers–New Brunswick, graduating in May 2028, working toward data science, analytics, AI/ML and product-minded engineering. I hold the Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate.

My best projects start with someone's actual problem: an older adult missing a dose, a family overpaying a medical bill. They end with a working system that person could use. Away from the keyboard, I've played cricket since I was eight.

HOW I WORK

Less decoration. More intent.

01

Technology is a lever, not the pitch. I define the pain point before reaching for a stack.

03

Test before trusting.

On Fambud, no release shipped without passing its tests, and real users still found bugs the tests missed.

04

Polish what matters.
